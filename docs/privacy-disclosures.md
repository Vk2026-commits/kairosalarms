# Privacy disclosure update

The `/privacy` notice describes the current landing-page and admin lead workflow rather than security-system monitoring data. It is linked visibly from the landing-page footer and beside contact disclosures in the lead form. Form links open a new tab to preserve entered information. The page includes an accessible browser-local Meta tracking choice, Global Privacy Control signal handling through the existing Pixel guard, and privacy contact options at inquiry@kairossecurity.com and 1-888-524-7678.

The preference only controls future site-controlled Meta events in the current browser; it does not erase previously sent data, disable first-party campaign attribution, or withdraw contact consent. Saving reloads the document to remove an already loaded Pixel. The privacy page itself does not trigger Meta PageView events. A failed browser-storage write is shown as a failure, not a saved opt-out.

Notice content is grounded in the checked-in application: lead form fields and contact consent, Supabase lead storage, Resend notifications, authorized Kairos/Vektiss follow-up access, CRM notes, advertising attribution, Meta events, and external font/error-diagnostic requests. It does not claim a fixed retention period, sale prohibition, statutory applicability, or comprehensive legal compliance. No cookie-consent banner, scheduled report, or ongoing monitor is added by this update.

The business should have its privacy owner or counsel review the notice and confirm its legal identity, retention/deletion practices, any additional offline/phone/SMS operations, and applicable state or regional requirements. An opt-out does not replace prior consent in jurisdictions that require it. Publication remains a separate user action in Lovable.

## Sources

[Meta Business Tools Terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/technology_terms) require prominent website disclosures of Pixel collection, sharing, use for measurement/advertising, and a mechanism to exercise choices. They separately require appropriate prior consent where applicable.

[FTC: Protecting Personal Information — A Guide for Business](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business) recommends mapping actual information flows, collecting only needed information, appropriate retention policies, controlled access, and provider oversight. These recommendations are not presented as practices already implemented by the business.

[AdChoices controls](https://youradchoices.com/control) provide an additional advertising-choice resource. [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/) explains Meta's own handling of information; external provider policies are separate from this website notice.
