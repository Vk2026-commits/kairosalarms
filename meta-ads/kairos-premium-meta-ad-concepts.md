# Kairos Security — Premium Meta Static Creative Pack

**Status:** Draft concepts for human review  
**Skill installed locally:** `.agent-skills/facebook-ad-creative-skill`  
**Primary placement:** Meta Feed — 4:5, 1080 × 1350  
**Campaign objective:** Lead generation through the existing security-options diagnostic

## What this pack is designed to do

This is a **premium, residential-security** creative system—not a discount-security template. It uses quiet confidence, architectural warmth, restrained copy, and editorial composition to make Kairos feel local, professional, and considered.

The concepts are deliberately different at the hypothesis level. They cover product clarity, offer/value, property-fit benefit, and local lifestyle. They are ready for image production after the team selects the strongest directions.

## Premium visual system

| Element         | Direction                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Palette**     | Deep burgundy `#470101` / near-black burgundy, warm ivory, muted stone, and controlled antique-gold highlights.                                                                                                        |
| **Typography**  | Plus Jakarta Sans for confident headlines; Inter for supporting information. Use two to three text levels only.                                                                                                        |
| **Photography** | Architectural home details, real installation moments, warm interior light against dusk-blue exteriors, and uncluttered residential settings.                                                                          |
| **Finish**      | Premium editorial—not fake luxury. Sculpted lighting, deliberate crops, tactile stone/wood/metal, restrained graphics, and generous negative space.                                                                    |
| **Avoid**       | Generic blue security shields, neon alarms, surveillance-camera close-ups as the sole subject, fake dashboard UI, fake reviews, stock-call-center imagery, aggressive sale badges, and cluttered specification sheets. |

## Production rules for all four concepts

- Keep all critical visual and copy elements inside the center **80%** of the 4:5 frame.
- Use a single dominant message per ad; Meta provides the CTA outside the creative, so no artificial button is required inside the image.
- Preserve the recognizable architecture and warm dusk mood of `src/assets/hero-home.jpg` whenever it is used as a reference.
- Preserve the natural installation setting and panel interaction of `src/assets/install.jpg`; do not invent vendor-specific hardware, logos, or unsupported panel features.
- Build separate 1:1 and 9:16 compositions later; do not crop the 4:5 master blindly.

**Dominant angle:** Minimal aesthetic / premium product clarity

- **Product anchor:** A refined modern home entrance at blue hour, based on the existing hero image; front door, doorway lighting, and architectural stone are the visual “product.”
